SECTION 1: ABOUT STARBUCKS
================================================================================

1.1 Company Overview
Starbucks Corporation is an American multinational chain of coffeehouses and roastery reserves headquartered in Seattle, Washington. Founded in 1971, Starbucks is the world's largest coffeehouse chain with over 35,000 locations in 84 countries. Our mission is to inspire and nurture the human spirit — one person, one cup and one neighborhood at a time.

1.2 Starbucks Rewards Program
Starbucks Rewards is our loyalty program where customers earn Stars with every purchase. Members earn 1 Star per dollar spent when paying with cash, credit/debit cards, or mobile wallets, and 2 Stars per dollar when paying with a registered Starbucks Card. Stars can be redeemed for free drinks, food, merchandise, and more. Reward levels include Green (25 Stars) and Gold (300 Stars) tiers.

1.3 Starbucks App Features
The Starbucks app allows customers to: order and pay ahead, earn and track Stars, access exclusive member offers, find nearby stores, manage Starbucks Card, and explore the full menu with nutritional information.

1.4 Drink Sizes
Starbucks offers the following drink sizes:
- Short (8 fl oz) - available for hot beverages only
- Tall (12 fl oz) - small size
- Grande (16 fl oz) - medium size
- Venti (20 fl oz for hot, 24 fl oz for cold) - large size
- Trenta (30 fl oz) - available for cold beverages only

1.5 Milk Options
Starbucks offers a variety of milk options including: Whole milk (default for most drinks), 2% milk (reduced fat), Nonfat milk, Half-and-half (Breve), Heavy whipping cream, Oat milk (+$0.70), Almond milk (+$0.70), Coconut milk (+$0.70), Soy milk (+$0.70).

SECTION 11: ALLERGEN & DIETARY INFORMATION
================================================================================

11.1 Allergen Information
Starbucks beverages and food items may contain or come in contact with allergens including: milk, eggs, fish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, and soy. Cross-contamination is possible. Customers with severe allergies should inform the barista.

11.2 Vegan Options
Vegan-friendly drinks can be made by:
- Requesting any non-dairy milk (oat, almond, soy, coconut) — +$0.70
- Removing whipped cream
- Confirming syrups are vegan (most classic syrups are vegan; some seasonal sauces may contain dairy)

Vegan Food Items:
- Rolled Oats (without honey)
- Some Refreshers and iced teas

11.3 Gluten-Free
Starbucks does not have a certified gluten-free kitchen. However, some naturally gluten-free items include: most beverages, some protein boxes (verify ingredients).

SECTION 14: BREWING METHODS & COFFEE EDUCATION
================================================================================

14.1 Espresso
Espresso is the foundation of most Starbucks coffee drinks. It's made by forcing hot water through finely-ground coffee under high pressure. This creates a concentrated shot with a layer of golden crema on top. Each espresso shot is approximately 1 fluid ounce.

14.2 Cold Brew vs. Iced Coffee
- Iced Coffee: Brewed hot then chilled. Refreshing and straightforward.
- Cold Brew: Steeped in cold water for 20 hours. Smoother, naturally sweeter, and less acidic than iced coffee. Also higher in caffeine per ounce.

14.3 Nitro Cold Brew
Cold Brew infused with nitrogen gas, served from a pressurized tap. Creates a creamy, smooth texture and a cascading effect similar to Guinness beer. Served without ice.

14.4 Pour Over
A slow, methodical brewing method where hot water is poured over ground coffee in a filter. Highlights delicate flavors in single-origin coffees.

14.5 Caffeine Content
- Tall Brewed Coffee: ~260mg caffeine
- Single Espresso Shot: ~75mg caffeine
- Grande Cold Brew: ~205mg caffeine
- Nitro Cold Brew (Grande): ~280mg caffeine
- Grande Matcha Latte: ~80mg caffeine
- Herbal Teas: 0mg (caffeine-free)

SECTION 16: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
================================================================================

Q: What is the most popular Starbucks drink?
A: The Caramel Macchiato, Pumpkin Spice Latte, and Iced Brown Sugar Oat Milk Shaken Espresso consistently rank as top sellers. The Pink Drink is also enormously popular.

Q: What is the strongest coffee at Starbucks?
A: The Venti Blonde Roast has the most caffeine (~475mg). Among cold drinks, Nitro Cold Brew in Grande has ~280mg. Venti Cold Brew has ~310mg.

Q: Does Starbucks have decaf options?
A: Yes! Any espresso drink can be made with decaf espresso shots. Herbal teas are naturally caffeine-free.

Q: What are dairy-free options at Starbucks?
A: Choose oat milk, almond milk, soy milk, or coconut milk (+$0.70). Remove whipped cream. Most Refreshers, teas, and cold brews are naturally dairy-free.

Q: Can I order half-caf?
A: Yes! You can order any espresso drink half-caf (half regular, half decaf espresso).

Q: What is a "dry" vs "wet" cappuccino?
A: Dry cappuccino = more foam, less steamed milk. Wet cappuccino = more steamed milk, less foam. Standard is in between.

Q: Does Starbucks have any sugar-free syrups?
A: Yes, sugar-free versions of Vanilla, Caramel, and Hazelnut syrups are available.

Q: How do I order a "secret menu" drink?
A: Secret menu items are customizations. Tell your barista what you want step-by-step (base drink + customizations) rather than just naming the secret drink.

Q: What is the cheapest drink at Starbucks?
A: A Short brewed coffee or a single espresso shot starts around $2.25. Iced coffee (Tall) is $2.95.

Q: Can I bring my own cup?
A: Yes! Bring your own clean, personal cup and receive a $0.10 discount on your beverage. Reusable cups cannot be used for Mobile Orders at this time.

Q: What is "Blonde Roast" espresso?
A: Blonde Roast is Starbucks' lightest espresso roast. It has a mellow, smooth, and subtly sweet flavor. It also has slightly more caffeine than darker roasts because light roasting preserves more caffeine.

Q: Can I order a Frappuccino without coffee?
A: Yes! Ask for a "Crème Frappuccino" which uses a non-coffee base. Options include Vanilla Bean, Strawberry, Matcha, Double Chocolaty Chip Crème, and more.

Q: What is cold foam?
A: Cold foam is non-fat milk frothed cold (not steamed). It floats on top of iced drinks and slowly blends as you sip for a creamy texture without warming the drink.

Q: How do I earn Starbucks Stars?
A: Earn 2 Stars per $1 spent using a registered Starbucks Card (physical or app). Earn 1 Star per $1 with other payment methods.

Q: What can I redeem Stars for?
A: 25 Stars = customize your drink for free | 100 Stars = free hot food or cold drink | 200 Stars = free handcrafted drink | 300 Stars = free merchandise | 400 Stars = select merchandise items.
